02 — About

The quiet
parts matter.

I'm more interested in how a site feels to use than how it looks in a screenshot.

Most small businesses don't need a marketing department. They need it handled.

I design and build websites for businesses that need to be found, trusted and called. Most of my work is for owners who would rather get on with the job than manage a website — so everything I ship is built to run quietly in the background and let them forget about it.

I work in code, not templates. Every build is hand-written, tested on real phones, and fast even on a weak connection in a small town. When something can be removed, I remove it — that's usually the difference between a site that feels considered and one that feels assembled.

I'm based in Patna, India, and work with clients across the US and Europe. Your morning is my evening, which means most replies land in your inbox before you've finished your first coffee.
